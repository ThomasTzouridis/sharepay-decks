The buyer picks Split, adds the others and sets each share. Everyone gets their own link and approves their share on their own card. Approving places a hold, not a charge.

02
Forward

Send the bill to whoever is paying

The buyer forwards the whole bill to a parent, partner or company. They pay on a secure link, no account, no card details shared. The buyer is never charged and the order still goes through.
